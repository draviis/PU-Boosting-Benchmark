\noindent\textit{Note.} OS: one-sample; TS: two-sample. Brackets give the 25th and 75th percentiles. Standalone reference times are seconds and high-dimensional times are minutes; incremental corrections in the final panel are milliseconds. Raw times are always retained in seconds. CPU: AMD EPYC 7543 32-Core Processor. Python 3.10.12. One library thread per worker; at most 20 case workers pinned to distinct physical cores on one exclusive node. Active concurrency decreases as cases finish. All methods share the same data within a case. Warm-up is discarded. Timers include internal scoring/threshold preparation and redirected fitting diagnostics. Corrected pipelines reuse the matching naive fit: standalone time adds the measured correction; the fit is not charged twice to the shared benchmark. Candidate counts and software versions are in the raw CSV. Candidate counts are validation candidates/checkpoints from the frozen selection hooks, not counts of separate trajectories; AdaPU can evaluate several stages from each beta trajectory. The two supervised references are separate comparators. The exploratory neural extension uses a distinct protocol and is excluded.
